Tributes

Words of love, remembrance and gratitude for Kent Germain.

To a father and friend

Kent was a man of strength, humour and quiet wisdom. He loved deeply, gave generously, and carried himself with dignity in every season of life. To those who knew him, his presence was a source of comfort, guidance and encouragement.

A life shaped by faith

His faith was central to the way he lived. Rooted in Rastafari and guided by love, kindness and resilience, Kent showed the strength of a life lived with purpose. He reminded others to stand tall, be true to themselves, and hold fast to community and family.

A legacy that remains

Though his journey has now passed into memory, Kent’s influence remains in the stories people tell, the lessons they carry and the love that still surrounds his family. His laughter, his warmth and his spirit continue to live on in the hearts of those who loved him.

A message of thanks

We thank everyone who has shared their love, prayers and memories. Your support has brought comfort during this time of loss and has reminded us of the impact Kent had on so many lives.

May his memory continue to inspire compassion, faith and unity in all who knew him.

With love from family and friends.
