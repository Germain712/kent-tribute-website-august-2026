Service Outline

  • Pall Bearers: Reuben (Son), Hellion Bruney, Bert Muhammed, Des Webb, Dominique Bird & Louis (Nephew)
  1. Entrance Song: Selassie Is The Chapel – Bob Marley
  2. Welcome & Opening Prayers: Kwadwo Ogunbiyi

  4. 1st Poem: “Go into your Father’s arms” – read by Sue Webb

  6. Eulogy: Uncle Sylus
  7. Song: Rasta Man Chant – Drummers
  8. Tributes: Sophie, Des Webb, Michelle Dawson, Wailers F.C.

  10. Final Farewell: Jenny
  11. Final Address & Farewell: Kwadwo Ogunbiyi
